## What this is

One sentence, 137 characters, pitching a startup idea that sounds ridiculous until you see the customers and the money:

> We rent out silence by the hour: a thick-walled van parks outside your flat and swallows the drilling next door while you work from home.

The odd thing being sold is silence, by the hour. Who pays: anyone working from home next to a construction site, on a video call while the drilling starts. Why it holds together: noise is a real daily pain, the van is booked like a taxi, and the price is an hour of quiet, not a promise.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, no network, deterministic) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
